Tutorial for Betacraft

1. search for %appdata%  betacraft->versions->Copy b1.7.3.jar on a descop and rename it to whatever you like "mod1". 
2. Open it with 7zip and also zeta.jar. Drag everything from zeta.jar to ("mod1")b.1.7.3.jar.
3. drag also to it("mod1") lang, mod_items, mod_Terrain, mod_mob folders and after that you put "mod1" in the versions folder.
4. now you go to betacraft and create new instance(for now normal beta 1.7.3) and click play button. Close betacraft.
5. In %appdate% betacraft you search for name of your newly created instance. there: resources->newsound->mob there you add beholder, demon, specter folders. And in  resources->newsound->random there you put steelForge folder.
6.Now open betacraft and click select version for your instance and change it to the "mod1"
7.Play

8*optional. In save add tutorial_world and play it to learn more
